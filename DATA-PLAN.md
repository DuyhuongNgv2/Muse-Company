## Full audit + fix-all (ordered by the user 2026-11-26 late evening: "Audit lỗi thì cho ae sửa luôn đi")

Root cause of the recurring "sitting merged into the chair / sitting like on the floor" defect was geometric, in `Office3D.tsx` only: avatar legs totalled 0.50 (thigh 0.27 + shin 0.23) while desk/meeting seat tops are 0.52 and the torso capsule hung 0.235 below the hip pivot, so no pose could satisfy hips-on-seat + feet-on-floor at once. Fixed at the root: legs lengthened (hip pivot 0.73, thigh 0.34, shin 0.36), torso shortened below the hip (0.12), seated hip height now derived per seat from the furniture's MEASURED seat top (+0.07, divided by each person's height scale) — desk/meeting chair 0.52, tea chair 0.485, sofas 0.53, garden bench 0.51, library chair 0.50, dining stool 0.50, fun-zone lounge chair 0.51. Relax spots that had no chair under them or faced into a backrest were moved/rotated onto the real furniture (lobby sofa, tea sofas — which also had their build rotation flipped to actually face inward, per the in-code intent — library chair, dining chair). Desk nameplates got a solid back so they read as real plates from both sides (content was already title-only via STAFF_TITLES). Typing arm angle re-solved for the new proportions (1.12 rad). Geometry verified numerically for every seat type × every staff height scale; close-up in-browser capture of individual desks was not possible in the sandbox (probe ops time out on the heavy scene under software WebGL, as in prior rounds) — the user's own close-zoom check on their machine remains the visual confirmation.
